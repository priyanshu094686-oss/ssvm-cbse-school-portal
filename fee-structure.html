Quarterly Payment Schedule

  • 1st Quarter (April, May, June): Payable by April 15
  • 2nd Quarter (July, August, September): Payable by July 15
  • 3rd Quarter (October, November, December): Payable by October 15
  • 4th Quarter (January, February, March): Payable by January 15

Payment Instructions & Modes

  • Fees can be deposited via Bank Demand Draft, Cheque, Online Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), or at the School Accounts Counter.
  • All receipts are issued with official serial numbers and school seals.
  • Fee concessions under RTE Act / sibling concessions are applicable as per school committee guidelines.
  • Class 10 CBSE Board registration and examination fees are collected separately as notified by CBSE.

Need Clarification on Fee Details?

Parents are advised to consult the school accounts counter during official visiting hours.

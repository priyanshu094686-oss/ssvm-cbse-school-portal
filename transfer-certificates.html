Procedure for Obtaining a Transfer Certificate

  1. Submit a written application in the prescribed format signed by the parent/guardian at least 15 days in advance.
  2. Obtain clearance certificates from the School Library, Science/Computer Laboratories, Sports Incharge, and Accounts Section.
  3. Clear all outstanding school dues up to the current academic quarter.
  4. The Transfer Certificate will be prepared, counter-verified by the Principal, and issued within 7 working days.

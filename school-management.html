Key Responsibilities

Functions of the School Managing Committee

Statutory responsibilities defined under CBSE Affiliation Bye-Laws.

📋

Academic Governance

Ensuring curriculum implementation adheres strictly to CBSE / NCERT guidelines and monitoring annual academic performance.

🛡️

Campus Safety & Child Protection

Supervising building safety, fire preparedness, clean drinking water, sanitation certificates, and child protection policies.

👥

Staff Welfare & Appointments

Overseeing that all teachers possess prescribed CBSE qualifications, service conditions, and continuous professional development.

Admissions (Classes 1 to 10)

Official admission procedure, eligibility guidelines, required documentation checklist, and admission enquiry registration for Saraswati Shishu Vidya Mandir.

📥 Download Admission Form
Academic Session 2026–2027

Admission Overview

Saraswati Shishu Vidya Mandir welcomes admission applications for Classes 1 to 9. Admissions to Class 10 are governed strictly by CBSE transfer guidelines and are subject to state-level registration approvals.

The admission process is transparent, inclusive, and strictly non-discriminatory, following the directives of the Right to Education (RTE) Act and the Central Board of Secondary Education.

Official Notice: Admission is granted solely based on verified eligibility and seat availability. The school does not accept any capitation fee or donation in any form.

Important Admission Timelines

  • Enquiry & Registration Opens: December 15, 2026
  • Document Submission Deadline: March 10, 2027
  • Verification & Interaction: March 15 to March 25, 2027
  • Session Commencement: April 01, 2027
Step-by-Step Procedure

How to Apply for Admission

A simple, transparent 4-step pathway to securing your ward's admission.

1

1. Registration

Obtain the admission registration form from the school office or fill out the online enquiry form below.

2

2. Document Check

Submit self-attested copies of birth certificate, previous report card, transfer certificate (TC), and Aadhaar card.

3

3. Interaction

Informal interaction with the student and parents to understand learning readiness and school ethos.

4

4. Confirmation

Fee deposition as per the official fee schedule and issuance of the student admission number.

Mandatory Documents Checklist

  • ✓ Birth Certificate: Issued by Municipal Corporation / Registrar of Births (Mandatory for Class 1).
  • ✓ Transfer Certificate (TC): Original TC counter-signed by Education Authority for Class 2 onwards.
  • ✓ Previous Mark Sheet: Report card copy of the last examination passed.
  • ✓ Aadhaar Card: Copy of student and parents' Aadhaar card.
  • ✓ Photographs: 4 recent passport-size photographs of the student.
  • ✓ Category Certificate: SC/ST/OBC/EWS certificate if applicable.

Holistic Educational Philosophy

Foundations of Panchmukhi Shiksha

At Saraswati Shishu Vidya Mandir, our curriculum harmonizes CBSE academic standards with all-round intellectual, physical, moral, and cultural development.

📖

Academic Rigor

Strict adherence to the NCERT curriculum for Classes 1 to 10 with conceptual clarity, activity-based learning, and regular periodic evaluations.

🧘

Yoga & Physical Wellbeing

Daily morning prayer, yoga, physical training (Sharirik), and sports to inculcate discipline, mental focus, and robust physical health.

🔬

Scientific Temper

Laboratory-driven science exploration, mathematics thinking, computer literacy, and environmental consciousness.

🪔

Sanskar & Patriotism

Nurturing moral values (Naitik Shiksha), respect for Indian culture, national pride, and selfless social responsibility.

Structured Learning Journey

Academic Wings (Classes 1 to 10)

Structured pedagogical progression designed to foster critical thinking and comprehensive learning.

🌱

Primary Wing (Classes 1–5)

Foundational literacy, numeracy, and sensory observation. Focus on languages (Hindi, English, Sanskrit), Mathematics, and Environmental Studies through joyful experiential learning.

  • Focus: Foundational Literacy & Numeracy
  • Evaluation: Continuous Classroom Observation
View Curriculum Details ➔
📘

Middle Wing (Classes 6–8)

Transition to analytical reasoning in Science, Mathematics, Social Sciences, Computer Studies, and Three-Language Formula with practical experiments and project work.

  • Focus: Conceptual & Analytical Skills
  • Evaluation: Periodic Tests & Mid-Terms
View Curriculum Details ➔
🎓

Secondary Wing (Classes 9–10)

Rigorous preparation for CBSE All India Secondary School Examination (AISSE). Focus on NCERT syllabus mastery, internal assessment, laboratory practicals, and exam discipline.

  • Affiliated Exam: CBSE AISSE Class 10
  • Assessment: Board Exam & Internal (20 Marks)

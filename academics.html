Academics & Curriculum (Classes 1–10)

CBSE-aligned curriculum framework, NCERT syllabus implementation, subject combinations, evaluation systems, and teaching methodology.

📚 Download Syllabus & Booklist
Educational Structure

Academic Wings & Progression

Providing tailored educational methods for each developmental stage of childhood and early adolescence.

Classes 1 to 5

Primary Wing (Foundational & Preparatory)

Focus on building core language competence (Hindi, English, Sanskrit), basic numeracy, environmental appreciation, art, music, physical fitness, and moral stories.

Core Subjects:
  • Hindi, English Language & Grammar
  • Mathematics & Mental Arithmetic
  • Environmental Studies (EVS) / General Science
  • Sanskrit (Introductory)
  • Moral Education (Naitik Shiksha) & Cultural Knowledge
  • Yoga, Physical Training (Sharirik), Art & Craft

Pedagogical Approach

Playful, activity-oriented learning with minimum rote memorization. Regular classroom evaluations without high-stakes examination pressure.

Classes 6 to 8

Middle Wing (Upper Primary)

Deepening scientific and analytical capabilities. Systematic introduction to discrete disciplines: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Civics, and Computer Science.

Core Subjects:
  • Language 1: Hindi (Course A/B)
  • Language 2: English Language & Literature
  • Language 3: Sanskrit
  • Mathematics
  • Science (Integrated Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Social Science (History, Geography, Political Science)
  • Computer Studies / IT Literacy
  • Work Experience, Health & Physical Education, Yoga

Pedagogical Approach

Inquiry-based learning, practical laboratory sessions, group discussions, and project presentations in accordance with NCERT guidelines.

Classes 9 & 10

Secondary Wing (CBSE AISSE Preparation)

Rigorous preparation for the CBSE All India Secondary School Examination (AISSE). Focus on NCERT syllabus mastery, internal assessment portfolios, lab practicals, and conceptual clarity.

CBSE Board Prescribed Subjects:
  • Language 1: English Language & Literature (Code 184)
  • Language 2: Hindi Course-A (Code 002) / Sanskrit (Code 122)
  • Mathematics: Standard (Code 041) / Basic (Code 241)
  • Science with Practical Work (Code 086)
  • Social Science (Code 087)
  • Co-Scholastic: Health & Physical Education, Art Education, SEWA

Board Evaluation Blueprint

Board Theory Exam: 80 Marks
Internal Assessment: 20 Marks (Periodic Tests 5M, Multiple Assessment 5M, Portfolio 5M, Subject Enrichment/Lab 5M).

Methodology

Teaching Methodology (Panchmukhi Shiksha)

An integrated educational approach combining five key dimensions of child growth.

🏃

1. Sharirik (Physical)

Physical fitness, athletics, yoga asanas, and drill practice for building bodily stamina, endurance, and agility.

🧘

2. Yogic (Mental Balance)

Pranayama, meditation, and concentration exercises to sharpen mental focus and emotional equilibrium.

🪔

3. Naitik (Moral & Ethical)

Stories of national heroes, value introspection, honesty, duty, respect for elders, and community responsibility.

🎶

4. Sangit (Aesthetic & Cultural)

Vocal and instrumental patriotic songs, devotional singing, and cultural arts fostering harmony and cultural identity.

📜

5. Sanskrit (Linguistic Heritage)

Recitation of inspiring shlokas and foundational Sanskrit grammar, enhancing language intellect and cultural rootedness.

💻

Modern Scientific Integration

Seamless integration of modern ICT tools, smart classroom presentations, and laboratory practicals with timeless values.

Examination & Assessment Scheme

Structured annual testing system adhering to CBSE Uniform Assessment guidelines.

About Saraswati Shishu Vidya Mandir

A comprehensive overview of our institutional history, educational vision, core values, objectives, and CBSE affiliation status.

Institutional Heritage

School Overview & Philosophy

Saraswati Shishu Vidya Mandir is a recognized educational institution affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi, providing continuous schooling for boys and girls from Class 1 to Class 10.

Inspired by the national ideals of Vidya Bharati Akhil Bharatiya Shiksha Sansthan, the school blends traditional Indian sanskars (values) with progressive CBSE curriculum guidelines. The institution is dedicated to developing disciplined, knowledgeable, and patriotic youth who are equipped to meet contemporary academic challenges with ethical fortitude.

Guiding Principles

Vision, Mission & Core Values

Articulating our institutional commitment towards quality education and character formation.

🎯

Our Vision

To nurture young minds into capable, morally upright, and intellectually vibrant individuals who remain firmly rooted in cultural heritage while excelling in science, technology, and national service.

🚀

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality, inclusive, and learner-centric education under the CBSE framework; to cultivate physical stamina through Yoga and sports; to instill moral integrity and scientific temper across all grades.

⚖️

Core Values

Satya & Dharma (Truth & Duty): Integrity in words and deeds.
Desh Prem (Patriotism): Devotion to the nation.
Seva (Selfless Service): Compassion for community.
Shram & Anushasan: Diligence and self-discipline.

Key Objectives

Institutional Goals

  • Provide rigorous academic instruction in all NCERT prescribed subjects from Classes 1 to 10.
  • Implement the continuous and comprehensive evaluation scheme in full conformity with CBSE regulations.
  • Maintain a disciplined, safe, and nurturing environment adhering to child protection guidelines.
  • Equip students with computer literacy, scientific exploration skills, and language competencies (Hindi, English, Sanskrit).
  • Inculcate daily habits of yoga, physical exercise, moral introspection, and cultural awareness.
